> you know what Kingo is?                                                       

● A relationship-based authorization system: relationships between subjects and
  resources, with subject-set rewrite rules that derive relationships from other
  relationships, and a Check hot path that answers whether a given subject
  holds a given relationship on a given resource. Four services split by load
  profile — Write, Read+Expand, Watch, Check — over a pure rewrite engine.

  The name comes from your 2005 security system.
