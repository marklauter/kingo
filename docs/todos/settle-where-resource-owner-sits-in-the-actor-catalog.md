# Settle where resource owner sits in the actor catalog

## Observation

[[actor-catalog]] lists resource owner under primary actors, and content creator under stakeholders, noting that a content creator owns resources held by a relying party. A content creator is therefore a resource owner that never touches Kingo. The primary-actor definition puts the actor at the boundary, so it excludes them.

Two readings:

- Resource owner is an abstract root, and its subtypes split across the actor and stakeholder types.
- The primary entry narrows to the owners who reach the boundary, and content creator stays a separate stakeholder.

## Done when

[[actor-catalog]] carries one reading, and the resource owner's goal and conditions sit under whichever entries survive.
